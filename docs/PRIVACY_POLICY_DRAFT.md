# Privacy Policy (Draft — Phase 6)

Sponsor is a recovery support app. Your privacy is fundamental.

- All data (mood logs, journal, conversations) is stored **only on your device**, encrypted with a passphrase you choose.
- We do not collect, transmit, or store your personal data on any server.
- AI runs entirely on your device; no prompts leave your device.
- No analytics or telemetry are collected by default.
- If you contact a sponsor, that message is sent via your device's SMS app — Sponsor does not intercept or store it.
- Crisis resources (988, SAMHSA) are links; calls/texts go through your carrier.

**Data loss warning:** If you forget your passphrase, your data cannot be recovered. There is no reset.

This is a draft for clinical/legal review before launch.
